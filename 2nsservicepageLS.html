i.

LLM Mention Audit

A monthly snapshot of which prompts mention you, which mention competitors, and where you're absent. We test 200+ prompts per industry across all four AI surfaces.

  • Sentiment + accuracy of citations

ii.

Generative Engine Optimisation

"GEO. Like SEO, but for the new search box."

We restructure your content, schema, and on-page signals so AI crawlers can extract, summarise, and cite your pages cleanly. Same craft as SEO. Different judges.

  • Schema and structured data
  • Question-led content architecture
  • Llms.txt, robots, and AI-bot rules
  • FAQ + How-To rewrites
iii.

Citation Engineering

"Earning the third-party mentions LLMs are trained on."

LLMs cite brands that are mentioned consistently across the open web. We engineer placements on the high-trust sources AI tools weight heaviest: tier-1 press, listicles, comparison pages, expert directories.

  • Tier-1 press mentions
  • "Best of [X]" listicle insertions
  • Comparison page placements
  • Q&A platforms (Reddit, Quora)
iv.

Brand Entity Strengthening

"Telling Google's knowledge graph who you are."

A clean Wikipedia entry, a verified Wikidata entity, consistent G-Knowledge Panel data, and a strong Google Business presence — these are the entity signals every AI surface reads from.

  • Wikipedia & Wikidata cleanup
  • Google Knowledge Panel claim
  • Schema entity linking
  • Cross-platform brand consistency
