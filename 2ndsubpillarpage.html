How to Reach Darjeeling: Flights, Trains, Cabs & Rentals from Every City

Darjeeling has no airport and no major railway station of its own. Here's exactly how to get there - by air, train, road, toy train, shared jeep, private cab and rental - from nine major Indian cities, with real fares.

Darjeeling sits at 6,710 ft in the Eastern Himalayas - which means there's no airport on top of the mountain and no railway station big enough to handle express trains. Your entry point is always somewhere on the plains, around 70 km below. From there, you climb. The good news: getting to that base station is easy from every Indian city with daily flights and overnight trains. The harder part is the final 3 hours up the hill - and how you choose to do that final stretch makes the biggest difference to your time, comfort and budget. This guide breaks down every single option, with current 2026 fares and the smart-saver moves we've learned from a decade of doing this drive.

01 · The overview

Three ways to reach Darjeeling - the basics

Every trip to Darjeeling involves two legs: getting to Siliguri (the gateway town in the plains), and then climbing 70-77 km up the mountain to Darjeeling town. The Siliguri leg can be done by air (Bagdogra), train (New Jalpaiguri / NJP) or road. The mountain leg is almost always by road - either private cab, shared SUV, or in a slow but iconic toy train.

The 90% rule
Around 9 out of every 10 travellers fly into Bagdogra Airport, take a pre-paid cab or shared SUV up the hill, and arrive at their Darjeeling hotel by evening. If you're not sure which mode to pick - that's the safest default.

02 · The fastest way

How to reach Darjeeling by air

Darjeeling does not have its own commercial airport. The nearest airport is Bagdogra Airport (IATA: IXB), located near Siliguri, around 68-70 km from Darjeeling town. From the moment you step off the plane to your hotel check-in, you're looking at around 4 hours total - 30 minutes to clear airport, 3-3.5 hours uphill drive.

Bagdogra is a domestic-with-limited-international airport operated by the Airports Authority of India. It's small but efficient, with daily direct flights from:

  • Delhi (DEL) - IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa Air - 2 hr flight time
  • Mumbai (BOM) - IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, Vistara - 2 hr 35 min
  • Bangalore (BLR) - IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air - 2 hr 50 min
  • Chennai (MAA) - IndiGo - 2 hr 30 min
  • Hyderabad (HYD) - IndiGo - 2 hr 15 min
  • Kolkata (CCU) - IndiGo, Air India - 55 min
  • Guwahati (GAU) - IndiGo - 50 min
  • Ahmedabad (AMD) - IndiGo (via Delhi/Kolkata) - 3 hr 30 min

Airport facilities

Pre-paid taxi counter (inside arrivals), shared SUV stand (100 metres outside the airport), restroom, food court, ATMs (HDFC, SBI), free Wi-Fi (limited), currency exchange. No metro or train connectivity from the airport.

How to go from Bagdogra to Darjeeling

Once you exit Bagdogra Airport, you have three options:

  1. Pre-paid taxi counter (inside arrivals) - private cab to Darjeeling for ₹3,500-4,500 (sedan) or ₹4,500-6,000 (SUV)
  2. Shared SUV stand (100 metres outside the airport gate) - shared Tata Sumo / Innova, ₹350-450 per seat, runs when full (6-8 passengers). Best for solo or couple travellers.
  3. Hotel-arranged pickup - most heritage hotels in Darjeeling will send a private driver. Often costs ₹500-1,000 more than pre-paid counter, but you skip the bargaining.
Smart saver
Skip the airport counter cabs (overpriced) and walk 100 metres outside the airport gate to the shared SUV stand. You pay ₹350-450 per seat instead of ₹3,500+. If you're a solo traveller or couple, you save ₹2,500-3,000 right there. The SUVs are clean, safe, and the same drivers run this route every day.

Other nearby airports (rarely used)

If Bagdogra flights are full or expensive, these are technically your alternatives:

  • Bhadrapur (BDP), Nepal - 55 km from Darjeeling. Has flights from Kathmandu but requires border crossing. Only useful for international travellers.
  • Biratnagar (BIR), Nepal - 100 km, similar story.
  • Kolkata (CCU) - 600+ km. Useful only if you want to take an overnight train onward, since it gives you better flight options.
  • Pakyong Airport (PYG), Sikkim - 100 km away. Limited daily flights, mostly to Kolkata.
Honest tip
99% of travellers should just fly into Bagdogra. The alternatives only make sense in extreme cases (Bagdogra closure, very specific Sikkim itineraries, or international entry via Nepal).

03 · The classic way

How to reach Darjeeling by train

Train is the most romantic, most affordable, and (for travellers from eastern India) the most popular way to reach Darjeeling. There are two trains stations to know about - New Jalpaiguri (NJP) for express trains, and the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (toy train) for the final scenic climb.

NJP is one of the most well-connected railway stations in eastern India. The station has pre-paid taxi counters, a shared SUV stand at the forecourt, retiring rooms, food stalls, and basic conveniences.

Top trains to NJP from major cities

  • Kolkata (Howrah) → NJP: Vande Bharat Express (22301) - 7.5 hr, fastest. Darjeeling Mail (12343) - 10 hr overnight, most popular. Padatik Express (12377) - 11 hr.
  • Delhi (NDLS) → NJP: NDLS-NJP Rajdhani Express (12424) - 22 hr. Bihar Sampark Kranti (12557) - 27 hr. Several other long-haul services.
  • Mumbai (LTT) → NJP: LTT-NJP Express - 50 hr. Most Mumbai travellers fly instead.
  • Bangalore (KSR) → NJP: Bangalore-Guwahati Express - 50+ hr (rarely used).
  • Patna (PNBE) → NJP: Numerous daily trains - 9-12 hr.
  • Guwahati (GHY) → NJP: Multiple daily services - 6-7 hr.

From NJP to Darjeeling

Same three options as Bagdogra:

  1. Shared SUV from the station forecourt - ₹300-400 per seat, 3-3.5 hours
  2. Private cab - ₹3,500-4,500
  3. Hotel pickup - typically ₹4,000-5,500
Smart saver
The overnight Darjeeling Mail from Kolkata reaches NJP around 8:00 AM. Walk straight to the shared SUV stand and you'll be in Darjeeling by 1:00 PM with the whole afternoon free. Total cost: ₹500-2,400 train + ₹350-400 SUV = under ₹3,000 from Kolkata.

The legendary toy train runs daily from NJP all the way up to Darjeeling. It's a UNESCO World Heritage railway and unquestionably scenic - but it takes 7-8 hours one way, which is the same time as the road journey plus a long pause for views. For most travellers, the better move is to take a road cab up the mountain and instead enjoy the much shorter Darjeeling-Ghoom-Darjeeling joyride later in your trip (2-hour round trip, ₹1,300-1,600).

If you do want the full NJP to Darjeeling toy train ride

  • Book through IRCTC at least 2-3 weeks in advance
  • Pick the steam engine if available (more atmospheric)
  • Bring snacks - food stops are limited
  • Avoid during monsoon - cancellations are common

Siliguri Junction (an alternative)

Siliguri Junction (SGUJ) is a slightly smaller station also located in the same town as NJP. A few trains terminate here instead. Distance to Darjeeling is similar - 77 km via NH-55. Most travellers just use NJP since it has more direct services from major cities.

04 · The scenic way

How to reach Darjeeling by road

The 70-77 km uphill road from Siliguri is one of India's most scenic mountain drives. The road climbs from ~400 ft at the plains to 6,710 ft at Darjeeling, crossing tea estates, the historic town of Kurseong, and Ghoom (India's highest railway station).

The two main routes from Siliguri

  • NH-55 (Hill Cart Road) - the original road built by the British. 77 km, parallels the toy train tracks for several stretches. More scenic but slightly longer. The standard tourist route.
  • NH-110 - 70 km via Pankhabari Road. Slightly shorter, fewer hairpin bends, popular with private cabs. Used by most shared SUVs.

Transport options from Siliguri/Bagdogra/NJP

Shared SUVs (Tata Sumo, Innova, Mahindra Bolero)

The cheapest and most local option. Shared SUVs run from Tenzing Norgay Bus Terminal (Siliguri), the NJP forecourt, and outside Bagdogra Airport. They depart when full (6-8 passengers), so you might wait 15-45 minutes. Cost: ₹300-450 per seat. Time: 3-3.5 hours. Driver makes 1-2 short tea stops along the way.

Private cabs

Booked through pre-paid counters or apps. Cost: ₹3,500-4,500 sedan / ₹4,500-6,000 SUV. Door-to-door, no waiting. Bookable through hotel, Uber Intercity (occasionally available), MakeMyTrip cabs, or local agencies.

WBSTC government bus

West Bengal State Transport Corporation runs daily buses from Siliguri to Darjeeling. Cost: ₹120-180. Time: 4-4.5 hours. Slower than shared SUVs and seats fill fast - not ideal for tourists.

Self-drive

Technically possible but we don't recommend it for first-timers. The road is narrow, full of hairpin bends, and shared with the toy train tracks in several stretches. Many sections are single-lane. Driver fatigue is a real risk. Stick to cabs or shared SUVs.

Travel time reality check
Google Maps shows 2 hours 30 minutes for Siliguri-Darjeeling. In real life, it's 3-3.5 hours minimum due to switchback turns, slow trucks, toy train crossings and a tea stop. Plan accordingly.

06 · The last mile

From the airport or station to your Darjeeling hotel

You've landed at Bagdogra or arrived at NJP. Now what? Here's the exact step-by-step.

From Bagdogra Airport to your hotel

  1. Collect baggage - belt usually clears in 15-20 minutes for IndiGo / Air India flights.
  2. Pick your transport at the exit:
    • Pre-paid taxi counter inside arrivals - private cab ₹3,500-4,500
    • Walk 100 metres outside for shared SUVs - ₹350-450 per seat
    • Hotel driver - if pre-booked, they'll be holding a placard
  3. The drive up - 3 to 3.5 hours. One tea/loo stop in Kurseong is standard. You'll see tea estates around 90 minutes in.
  4. Arrival in Darjeeling - most hotels are clustered around Mall Road, Gandhi Road, Jalapahar and Robertson Road. Driver will usually drop you within walking distance of your specific hotel. Some narrow lanes don't allow vehicles all the way - be ready to walk the last 100-300 metres.

From NJP Station to your hotel

Same process, just replace airport with station. The shared SUV stand is on the right as you exit the main station building. Pre-paid taxi counter is inside. Plan to arrive at NJP by 9 AM to comfortably reach Darjeeling by 1-2 PM.

Reach Darjeeling before dark
Mountain roads have minimal street lighting and most homestays are tucked into narrow lanes that are hard to navigate at night. Aim to reach Darjeeling town before 6 PM in summer / 5 PM in winter.
Pro tip
Save your hotel's phone number and the Google Maps pin in offline mode. Most cab drivers know the major hotels by name, but homestays in offbeat areas require precise directions - they're often known by the owner's name, not a street address.

07 · Inside Darjeeling

Local transport within Darjeeling town

Once you're in Darjeeling, getting around is easier than you'd think. Most of the main tourist circuit is walkable.

Walking

Darjeeling town is built across steep ridges. Mall Road, Chowrasta, Observatory Hill, Mahakal Temple, Glenary's, Keventer's and Habeeb Mullick handicrafts are all within 15-20 minutes of each other on foot. Wear shoes with grip - the streets are steep and can be slippery in morning dew or rain.

Local taxis

Standard yellow-and-black taxis are everywhere. They operate on point-to-point fares (not meters). Sample fares:

Shared jeeps (locally called "shared")

The cheapest way to get around. Shared jeeps depart from Chowk Bazaar (near Clubside taxi stand) for routes to Ghoom, Sonada, Kurseong, Mirik and other nearby towns. Cost: ₹30-200 per seat depending on distance. Great for budget travellers.

Tiger Hill morning jeeps

Special shared jeeps run pre-dawn (3:30-4:00 AM) for the Tiger Hill sunrise tour. They cost ₹200-300 per person round trip and include the Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery stops on the way back. Book a day in advance through your hotel.

7-point and 10-point tour packages

Local taxi unions sell pre-packaged sightseeing tours:

  • 7-point tour: Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Ghoom Monastery, Peace Pagoda, HMI, Zoo, Happy Valley Tea Estate - around ₹2,500-3,500 per cab (5-7 hours)
  • 10-point tour: Adds Rock Garden, Ganga Maya Park, Tibetan Refugee Centre - ₹3,500-4,500

Ola, Uber, Rapido

Important: Neither Ola nor Uber nor Rapido operate inside Darjeeling town. The local taxi union doesn't allow them. Bagdogra and Siliguri have limited Ola/Uber availability, but for everything inside Darjeeling, you'll use local taxis, hotel cabs or shared jeeps.

Smart saver
For a full day of sightseeing, hiring a private cab for 6-8 hours (₹2,500-3,500) is almost always cheaper than booking point-to-point rides. Negotiate at Clubside taxi stand or through your hotel - both have fair rates.

08 · The rentals

Car, scooter & bike rentals in Darjeeling

If you want flexibility and like driving yourself, rentals are available - but with serious caveats. Mountain roads, narrow lanes, hairpin bends and unfamiliar terrain mean self-drive is best for experienced hill drivers only.

🚗

Self-drive car

₹2,500-4,500 / day

Hatchbacks & SUVs from Zoomcar, Revv (Bagdogra/Siliguri pick-up). Driving licence + security deposit (₹5K-10K) required. Fuel extra.

🚔

Scooter rental

₹500-800 / day

Activa & Jupiter from local agencies near Chowk Bazaar. Helmet included. Best for short trips around town - not for Tiger Hill or Mirik.

🏌

Motorbike rental

₹1,000-1,800 / day

Royal Enfield & KTM available in Siliguri (pick up there, not in Darjeeling). Ideal for the road from Siliguri to Sandakphu / Sikkim.

Where to rent

  • Self-drive cars: Zoomcar & Revv have pickup points at Bagdogra Airport and Siliguri. Inside Darjeeling town, very few self-drive options exist - the town's narrow lanes don't reward self-drive tourists.
  • Scooters & bikes: Local rental shops near Chowk Bazaar and Robertson Road. Ask your hotel - most have a tied-up vendor.
  • With-driver SUVs: Most travellers rent a cab with driver for the full duration of their stay. This is the smartest middle ground - flexibility without the stress of mountain driving.

What you'll need

  • Valid Indian driving licence (international travellers need an International Driving Permit)
  • Security deposit - ₹1,500-2,000 for scooters, ₹5,000-10,000 for cars
  • Original government ID
  • Helmet (for scooter/bike) - usually provided
Honest take
For 90% of travellers, hiring a cab with driver (₹2,500-3,500 per day) is smarter than self-drive. You sit back, enjoy the views, the driver handles the hairpin bends, and you don't worry about parking or the narrow Mall Road approach. Save self-drive for experienced hill drivers.

12 · The questions

Frequently asked questions

Which is the nearest airport to Darjeeling?
The nearest airport to Darjeeling is Bagdogra Airport (IATA: IXB), located near Siliguri in West Bengal, around 68-70 km from Darjeeling town. The road distance via NH-110 is about 3 to 3.5 hours by cab. Bagdogra has direct daily flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad and Guwahati.
Which is the nearest railway station to Darjeeling?
New Jalpaiguri Railway Station (NJP), located near Siliguri, is the nearest major railway station to Darjeeling. It's around 70 km away and takes 3 to 3.5 hours by road. Darjeeling has its own heritage railway station served by the UNESCO Darjeeling Himalayan Railway toy train from NJP, but the 88 km journey takes 7-8 hours one way.
How far is Bagdogra from Darjeeling?
Bagdogra Airport is around 68-70 km from Darjeeling town via NH-110 and Hill Cart Road. The drive takes 3 to 3.5 hours in normal weather and up to 5 hours during monsoon or peak season traffic.
What is the cheapest way to reach Darjeeling?
The cheapest way is by train to NJP followed by a shared SUV up the hill (₹300-400 per seat). From Kolkata, the overnight Darjeeling Mail in sleeper class plus a shared jeep costs around ₹1,000 per person. The WBSTC government bus from Siliguri to Darjeeling is ₹120-180 but slower.
Is Ola or Uber available in Darjeeling?
No. Neither Ola nor Uber nor Rapido operate inside Darjeeling town. The local taxi union doesn't allow them. You'll use local yellow-and-black taxis, shared jeeps from designated stands, hotel-arranged cabs, or pre-booked private cars. Bagdogra and Siliguri have limited Ola/Uber availability but most travellers use pre-paid taxis from the airport counter.
Is the toy train a good way to reach Darjeeling?
The full NJP to Darjeeling toy train ride takes 7-8 hours one way and is mainly worth it if you specifically want the heritage railway experience. For most travellers, it's smarter to take a 3.5 hour road cab up the mountain and instead enjoy the much shorter Darjeeling-Ghoom-Darjeeling toy train joyride (2 hours) once you're in town.
Can I self-drive to Darjeeling?
Yes, but only if you're an experienced hill driver. The road from Siliguri is narrow, has many hairpin bends, and is shared with the toy train tracks in several stretches. First-time hill drivers should always hire a private cab with driver instead. Self-drive cars can be rented from Zoomcar and Revv at Bagdogra or Siliguri.
Are there direct flights to Darjeeling?
No, Darjeeling does not have its own commercial airport. The nearest airport is Bagdogra (IXB), 68 km away. From Bagdogra, you take a cab or shared SUV up the mountain. Major airlines flying to Bagdogra include IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa Air and Vistara.
How long does it take to reach Darjeeling from Bagdogra Airport?
The drive from Bagdogra to Darjeeling takes 3 to 3.5 hours in normal conditions, covering 68-70 km via NH-110 and Hill Cart Road. During monsoon (June-September) or peak season weekends, the journey can take 4-5 hours due to traffic and weather.
Should I take a private cab or shared SUV from the airport?
If you're a solo traveller or couple, a shared SUV (₹350-450 per seat) is far cheaper than a private cab (₹3,500-4,500). If you're a family of 3-4 with luggage, a private cab works out similar in cost and gives door-to-door comfort. For elderly travellers or those with heavy luggage, always go private.
Is Vande Bharat available from Kolkata to Darjeeling?
There's no Vande Bharat directly to Darjeeling, but the Howrah-NJP Vande Bharat Express (22301) is the fastest train from Kolkata to the Darjeeling region. It takes 7.5 hours and runs daily, departing Howrah at 5:55 AM and reaching NJP at 1:25 PM. From NJP you take a 3.5 hour shared SUV or private cab up to Darjeeling.
Are pre-paid taxis safe at Bagdogra and NJP?
Yes. The pre-paid taxi counters inside Bagdogra Airport arrivals and at NJP station are run by the local taxi union and are completely safe. They issue a printed slip with the driver's name, vehicle number and your destination. Avoid unauthorised taxi touts outside the airport gate who quote inflated rates.
