Digital PR is not
your dad's PR.

Traditional PR is about magazine covers, press releases, and quarterly events. Digital PR is about online authority, search rankings, and AI citations. Same craft. Different output.

— Traditional PR

Built for the front page of yesterday's newspaper.

  • Optimised for impressions and brand awareness
  • Print-first, online second
  • Deliverables measured in column inches
  • Press releases blasted to a wire service
  • Event-driven (launches, fundraises, anniversaries)
  • Often disconnected from SEO and AI strategy
— Digital PR (what we do)

Built for Google, ChatGPT, and the customer reading on a phone.

  • Optimised for backlinks, brand mentions, and AI citations
  • Online-first — the URL is the deliverable
  • Measured in DR, links, and search authority
  • Targeted pitches to individual journalists
  • Story-driven (data, expertise, contrarian takes)
  • Wired into your SEO and GEO strategy from day one

Five campaign
types we run.

Different stories need different vehicles. We pick the right one for your industry, your founder, and the kind of authority you're trying to build.

— No. 01

Data Studies

We commission proprietary research, surveys, or data analyses, then pitch the findings to journalists who cover your space. Hardest-working PR vehicle ever invented.

"Spocket analysed 50,000 dropshipping orders. Here's what's selling in 2026."

— No. 02

Reactive PR & Newsjacking

When a story breaks in your category, we get your founder quoted within hours. The fastest way to land Tier 1 placements — but only if you can move at journalist speed.

"OpenAI just released a new model — here's what fintech founders should do this week."

— No. 03

Founder Thought Leadership

A consistent op-ed and bylined-article programme that turns your founder into a recognised voice in their category. The slowest PR play. Also the most defensible.

"How we built canva.in to DR 70 in 12 months — and why it changed how we think about TLD launches."

— No. 04

Expert Commentary & HARO

We position your team's experts as the go-to source for journalists. Daily monitoring, fast pitching, and pre-written quote libraries that make reporters' jobs easy.

"For comment on India's new digital payment regulations — Subham, founder of [client]."

— No. 05

Product Launches & Milestones

When you actually have news (a launch, fundraise, milestone), we run a coordinated press push that maximises landings instead of "press release into the void."

"Series B announcement coordinated across TechCrunch, Mint, and 8 industry outlets in a 4-hour window."

— No. 06

Awards & Recognition Plays

We map every relevant award, list, and "30 under 30" your founder qualifies for, then run a structured submission programme. The links and brand-trust ROI is enormous.

"Three awards submissions in Q1 → Two wins → Forty-six press mentions citing the wins."

Four steps from
story to press.

The same framework whether the campaign is a data study or a reactive newsjack. The speeds change. The structure doesn't.

i.

Story Discovery

We mine your data, your team's expertise, and your industry's trending conversations for the angle a journalist would actually open the email for.

ii.

Asset Build

We produce the linkable asset — landing page, study, hero data viz — designed to be quoted, screenshotted, and cited without modification.

iii.

Targeted Outreach

No press wires. We hand-pick journalists by beat, byline, and recency, then send personalised pitches built around what *they* cover.

iv.

Compound & Track

Every placement gets logged. We chase syndications, request link upgrades on brand mentions, and feed the campaign back into AI citation work.
