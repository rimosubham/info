The whole team · no hidden founders

Offbeat Peak, all of it.

This is everyone. No "department of" or "head of" pretending to be bigger. Six people, four time zones depending on where we're traveling, one WhatsApp group that never stops pinging.

Co-founder · Lead trip designer

Arjun Mehta

the one on whatsapp at 11pm

Ex-IIT, ex-consultant, ex-corporate hater. Quit his job in 2023 after the Rajasthan trip. Now designs custom itineraries, reads every single form submission, and will probably be your main point of contact. Has been to 31 of our 140+ destinations personally.

Specialties
Himachal Uttarakhand Custom trips Solo travel

Ex-hotel industry, recovering perfectionist. Runs every operation — payments, bookings, logistics, the homestay network. When roads close or stays fall through, she's the one who rebooks in 4 hours flat. 43 times and counting.

Specialties
North Bengal Sikkim Group trips Homestay partnerships
Known for: A spreadsheet that tracks every homestay's rain-roof inspection. And being at Tirthan right now.
