Booking & pricing

6 questions
What does a trip actually cost — end to end?

Planning fees: Guide ₹1,500, Full Plan from ₹5,500 per person, Concierge from ₹12,000 per person.

On-ground costs (stays, food, transport, guides): typically ₹1,500–₹4,500 per day per person depending on comfort level. Our average 7-day trip costs travelers ₹42k–₹65k per person all-in.

We break down every cost transparently during planning. No hidden fees. No "convenience charges". No surprise add-ons.

How do payments work?

₹1,500 Guide fee: paid after you see the initial plan. Full Plan & Concierge: 30% on confirmation, 40% 30 days before trip, 30% 7 days before.

UPI, credit card, bank transfer, and international cards all accepted. We use Razorpay for processing — same infrastructure as most Indian startups.

What's your refund policy?

45+ days before: Full refund.
30–45 days: 80% refund.
15–30 days: 50% refund.
7–15 days: 30% refund.
Under 7 days: Travel credit valid 12 months.

Medical emergencies (with documentation): full travel credit, no questions. Full refund policy at /legal.

Is the 20% launch discount real?

Yes, and it's auto-applied to all itineraries and group trips till May 31, 2026. After that, prices reset to full. Not a fake deadline.

Can I book flights through you?

No — you'll always get better rates booking directly. But we'll recommend the best airports, routes, and timings in your itinerary, and tell you which dates will save you money.

Do you do gift vouchers?

Yes. Vouchers starting at ₹5,000, valid 18 months, redeemable against any trip or itinerary. Email hello@offbeatpeak.com with "gift voucher" in the subject line.

Custom trips

5 questions
What if I don't know where I want to go?

These are our favorite trips to design. Fill the form with what kind of experience you want (quiet, adventurous, slow, solo) and we'll come back with 2–3 destination options that match.

How long does planning take?

First draft: 48 hours. Revisions: 24 hours each. From form to final itinerary: usually 5–7 days total for Full Plan trips. Our record is a full Himachal custom itinerary in 34 hours.

How many revisions do I get?

Guide: 1 free revision.
Full Plan: unlimited revisions until you approve.
Concierge: unlimited revisions + a 1:1 planning call anytime during the process.

Can a group of us split costs / stays?

Absolutely. We do this often for big groups — one family in a heritage stay, friends in a homestay nearby, meeting up during the day. Works beautifully for multi-generational trips. Group of 4+? 5th guide/plan is free.

Can I just get a few recommendations without a full itinerary?

Yes. Mini-consultation: ₹799 for a 30-minute WhatsApp or voice call. We give you a shortlist of homestays, experiences, or route ideas. You go from there. Perfect for travelers who want local intel but will DIY the booking.

Group trips

4 questions
How big are the groups?

Maximum 12 travelers. Always. Workation trips are capped at 10. No exceptions — this is why they work.

I'm introverted / not great with strangers. Will I be okay?

Most travelers say this beforehand. And every single one ends up in the WhatsApp group after. We pair introverts together, build in plenty of solo time, and nobody will push you to "participate".

What's included in group trip prices?

All accommodation, local transport from starting city, all meals except 2 "explore on your own" dinners, guide fees, insurance, permits, entry fees.

Not included: flights, alcohol, personal shopping, spa/massage upgrades.

Are children allowed on group trips?

Currently 18+. We're planning a family-focused departure in 2027 — join our newsletter to get first access.

Safety & solo travel

5 questions
Is this safe for solo women travelers?

Yes — and we take it very seriously. 186 solo women travelers in the last 18 months. Zero incidents.

Our Uttarakhand, Sikkim, and Himachal routes use female-run homestays, female guides where available, and a local guardian network — a verified contact in every town you visit.

What's the "solo women safety kit"?

Free with every solo women trip. Includes: personal safety alarm, local SIM with unlimited data, satellite GPS tracker (rental), 24/7 WhatsApp panic contact, and printed safety cards in the local language for showing drivers/locals.

Do you provide travel insurance?

Full Plan and Concierge trips: insurance included (partnered with ICICI Lombard, covers medical evacuation, trip cancellation, baggage loss). Guide-only travelers: we'll recommend a policy — we don't take commissions.

What altitudes should I be worried about?

Anything above 2,500m needs acclimatisation. We build rest days into high-altitude itineraries (Spiti, Ladakh, Sikkim mountains). If you have heart or lung conditions, we'll route you to lower-altitude alternatives. Zero altitude sickness emergencies so far.

What's your food safety approach?

Our listed homestays and cafés all use filtered water and boiled vegetables. We've had fewer than 4 minor stomach incidents per 100 travelers over 2 years. Vegetarian, vegan, jain, gluten-free — all accommodated with advance notice.

On-ground support

4 questions
What happens if roads close or weather ruins my trip?

Welcome to the Himalayas. Every itinerary has backup routes built in. Full Plan travelers get rebooking at zero extra cost — we've done this 43 times across 2 years. Zero travelers stranded.

We'll call you, rebook stays, arrange alternative routes — usually within 4 hours of the disruption.

Do you provide guides on-ground?

Full Plan: local guide introductions in every town (not a full-time guide, but a named human with a phone number).
Concierge: dedicated trip coordinator with you or on call throughout.
Guide-only: recommendations for local guides you can contact directly.

Is there WhatsApp support during my trip?

Yes, 24/7 for Full Plan and Concierge travelers. Priya or Arjun will be your direct contact. Guide-only travelers get WhatsApp during business hours.

What if I need medical help?

We maintain a doctor directory for every destination — tourist-friendly, English-speaking, roughly priced. Full Plan includes insurance with medical evacuation. Call us and we'll coordinate immediately.
