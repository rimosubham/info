What's the difference between "Guide" and "Full Plan"?

Guide (from ₹599) is the PDF itinerary — you book your own stays and transport. Full route, timings, maps, hidden spots.

Full Plan (from ₹38k) is everything booked for you — stays, transport, local guide intros, WhatsApp support during your trip.

Can I customize a ready-made itinerary?

Absolutely. Upgrade to Full Plan and we adjust the itinerary to your dates, pace, and budget. Or download the Guide and treat it as a starting map.

Are prices per person or for the full trip?

The Guide is a flat fee — one PDF, share with your group. The Full Plan price is per person (stays, transport, guide fees, insurance included).

What if weather or roads close during my trip?

Welcome to the Himalayas. Every itinerary has backup routes built in. Full Plan travelers get rebooking at zero cost — we've done it 43 times, zero travelers stranded.

Do you book flights?

No. You'll get better rates direct. But we recommend airports, best routes, and dates that'll save you money.

Is the 20% launch offer real?

Yes. Auto-applied till May 31, 2026. After that, back to full price. Not a fake deadline.
