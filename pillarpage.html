02 · How to pick

How to choose the right number of days

If you've never been to Darjeeling before, choose 3 days for the classic experience, 5 days if you want both the famous spots and one offbeat village, 7 days if you're combining with Sikkim. Avoid 1-day or 2-day trips unless you live in Siliguri or Kolkata and absolutely cannot extend.

Our honest recommendation
3 days minimum, 5 days ideal. Anything less and you'll miss either the toy train or an offbeat village. Anything more than 7 days starts repeating experiences unless you specifically add the Sandakphu trek or extend into Sikkim.

03 · 2-day plan

2-day Darjeeling itinerary - the quick weekend escape

2 Days
The weekend run
From Kolkata / NJP
Best for

Office workers from Kolkata who can only get a 3-day weekend (Friday-Sunday). Realistically Tiger Hill + 7-point + Mall Road is all you'll fit. Skip if you want the toy train or any offbeat village.

Day 1Arrival & Mall Road evening

Take the overnight Darjeeling Mail from Kolkata or a morning flight to Bagdogra. Reach NJP/Bagdogra by morning, shared SUV up the mountain - in Darjeeling by 1 PM. Check into a mid-range hotel near Mall Road. Late lunch, freshen up. Late afternoon walk on Mall Road and Chowrasta. Sundown at Observatory Hill. Dinner at Glenary's.

Stay: Mall Road area (Sumi Yashshree / Hotel Sonar Bangla) Eat: Glenary's, Keventer's hot chocolate Transport: Shared SUV from NJP / Bagdogra
Day 2Tiger Hill + 7-point + departure

Pre-dawn pickup at 4 AM. Tiger Hill sunrise, Batasia Loop, Ghoom Monastery on the way back. Breakfast at Keventer's. Continue the 7-point circuit - Japanese Peace Pagoda, HMI & Zoo, Tenzing Rock, Happy Valley Tea Estate, Tibetan Refugee Centre. Late lunch at Kunga Restaurant. Hop on the 3:30 PM shared SUV back down. Reach NJP/Bagdogra by 7 PM for night flight or overnight train to Kolkata.

Stay: Travel night - sleeper train or flight Eat: Keventer's breakfast, Kunga lunch Transport: Shared jeep for Tiger Hill (₹200-300/person)

04 · 3-day plan

3-day Darjeeling itinerary - the classic (most popular)

3 Days
Tiger Hill + toy train + 7-point
Most popular
Best for

First-time visitors who want the complete classic Darjeeling experience. The single most-booked Darjeeling itinerary. Covers everything famous without rushing.

Day 1Arrival, Mall Road, settle in

Arrive Bagdogra/NJP morning, shared SUV/cab up. Check into Mall Road area hotel. Lunch. Slow walk on Mall Road and Chowrasta. Visit the Tibetan Refugee Self-Help Centre (closes 4:30 PM). Sundown at Observatory Hill. Dinner at Glenary's or Kunga.

Stay: Mall Road / Gandhi Road mid-range Eat: Glenary's / Kunga Transport: Walk + short cab
Day 2Tiger Hill sunrise + classic 7-point tour

4 AM pickup → Tiger Hill sunrise → Batasia Loop → Ghoom Monastery. Back for breakfast at Keventer's. Continue 7-point - Peace Pagoda, HMI & Zoo, Tenzing Rock, Happy Valley Tea Estate. Lunch at Kunga. Afternoon: visit Lloyd Botanical Garden or Bengal Natural History Museum if you have time. Evening on Mall Road. Dinner at Hot Stimulating Cafe (thenthuk).

Stay: Same Mall Road hotel Eat: Keventer's, Kunga, Hot Stimulating Cafe Transport: Shared jeep (Tiger Hill) + private cab (7-point)
Day 3Toy train + Rock Garden + departure

8 AM toy train joyride (Darjeeling-Ghoom-Darjeeling, 2 hours, book Day 1 evening). 11 AM brunch at Sonam's Kitchen. Drive to Rock Garden & Ganga Maya Park. Late lunch at Penang Restaurant. 4 PM shared SUV down to NJP/Bagdogra. Night train or flight onward.

Stay: Travel night Eat: Sonam's Kitchen, Penang Transport: Toy train (book on IRCTC) + cab + SUV

4-day Darjeeling itinerary - classic + one offbeat day

4 Days
Classic + Lamahatta day trip
Balanced pick
Best for

Travellers with one extra day. The 4-day version is the smart upgrade - you get the entire classic 3-day plan plus a full offbeat day to Lamahatta + Chunnu Falls + Tinchuley. Most rewarding "additional day" you can spend.

Same as 3-day plan Day 1 - arrive, settle, Mall Road walk, Tibetan Refugee Centre, Observatory Hill sunset, Glenary's dinner.

Stay: Mall Road hotelEat: Glenary's

06 · 5-day plan

5-day Darjeeling itinerary - the deep dive (our top pick)

5 Days
Classic + Mirik + offbeat overnight
Our top pick
Best for

The version we recommend most often. Couples, slow travellers, photographers, anyone with 5 days to spend. Combines the full classic Darjeeling with a Mirik day trip and one night in an offbeat homestay - which is what turns this into a genuine memory rather than a tour-package trip.

Day 1Arrival & Mall Road

Arrive, settle, slow Mall Road afternoon, Tibetan Refugee Centre, Observatory Hill sunset, dinner at Glenary's.

Stay: Mall Road heritage or mid-rangeEat: Glenary's
Day 2Tiger Hill + 7-point tour

Pre-dawn Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Ghoom Monastery, Keventer's breakfast. Complete 7-point circuit. Late lunch at Kunga. Evening Mall Road.

Stay: Same hotelEat: Keventer's, Kunga
Day 3Toy train + Happy Valley + Rock Garden

8 AM toy train joyride. Self-walk through Happy Valley Tea Estate with tasting. Lunch at Sonam's Kitchen. Afternoon: Rock Garden & Ganga Maya. Sunset chai at Tea Garden Viewpoint. Dinner at Hot Stimulating Cafe.

Stay: Same hotelEat: Sonam's, Hot Stimulating Cafe
Day 4Mirik day trip

7 AM start. Drive to Mirik via Pashupati Nagar. Sumendu Lake boating before tour buses arrive. Bokar Monastery on the hill, orange orchards (winter), shopping in Pashupati. Late lunch at a Mirik viewpoint cafe. Return to Darjeeling by 6 PM via Tabakoshi for tea-garden river views. Dinner at Singalila Restaurant for a proper Nepali thali.

Stay: Same Darjeeling hotelEat: Mirik cafe + SingalilaTransport: Full-day cab ₹3,500-4,500
Day 5Offbeat overnight - Lamahatta or Tinchuley

Check out from town by 9 AM. Drive to Lamahatta or Tinchuley (or Lepchajagat if you want the silent-forest option). Settle into a family homestay - pluck tea leaves, walk forest trails, eat a home-cooked Nepali meal. Sunset at Gumbadara or Hawa Ghar viewpoint. Sleep early.

Day 6 morning: Slow breakfast at the homestay, then 9 AM departure to Bagdogra/NJP for afternoon flight or evening train onward.

Stay: Offbeat village homestay (₹1,800-3K all meals)Eat: Home-cooked NepaliTransport: Private cab + return shared SUV

07 · 7-day plan

7-day Darjeeling + Sikkim itinerary

7 Days
Darjeeling + Gangtok + Tsomgo
Combined circuit
Best for

Travellers with a full week. Darjeeling and Gangtok are just 4-5 hours apart by road via the Teesta River route - perfect to combine. Two distinct cultural experiences in one trip.

Day 1-3Classic Darjeeling (compressed)

Same as the 3-day classic plan above - arrive, Mall Road, Tiger Hill, 7-point, toy train, Rock Garden. By end of Day 3, you've done the essential Darjeeling.

Day 4Drive to Gangtok via Teesta

9 AM check out from Darjeeling. Drive to Gangtok (~4-5 hours, 100 km via Peshok Road and Rangpo). Stop at Teesta Confluence (where the Rangeet meets Teesta) for photos. Reach Gangtok by 2 PM. Check into MG Marg area hotel. Evening walk on MG Marg, dinner at Taste of Tibet (Gangtok).

Stay: MG Marg area hotel, GangtokEat: Taste of TibetTransport: Private cab Darjeeling-Gangtok ₹3,500-4,500
Day 5Gangtok sightseeing

Rumtek Monastery (one of the most important Tibetan Buddhist monasteries), Hanuman Tok viewpoint, Ganesh Tok, Tashi Viewpoint, Banjhakri Falls. Lunch at a viewpoint cafe. Afternoon at Enchey Monastery or shopping on MG Marg. Cable car ride if time permits.

Stay: Same Gangtok hotelEat: Local Sikkimese restaurants
Day 6Tsomgo Lake + Baba Harbhajan Mandir

Permit-required excursion to Tsomgo Lake (3-4 hours up). Yak rides at the lake, visit Baba Harbhajan Mandir, optional drive up to Nathula border (if open and permits available). Return to Gangtok by evening. Dinner at MG Marg.

Stay: Same Gangtok hotelEat: MG Marg restaurantsTransport: Permit-only cab from Vajra Stand ₹5K shared
Day 7Return to Bagdogra

Morning check out, drive Gangtok → Bagdogra (~4 hours, 125 km). Lunch en route. Afternoon flight onward.

Stay: Travel dayTransport: Shared SUV or private cab

08 · 10-day plan

10-day Darjeeling + Sandakphu trek itinerary

10 Days
Classic Darjeeling + Sandakphu trek
For trekkers
Best for

Trekkers and adventure travellers. The full Sandakphu-Phalut trek is one of India's most scenic high-altitude treks - four of the world's five highest peaks visible from a single ridge. Combine with classic Darjeeling on either end. Best done October-November or March-April.

Day 1Arrive in Darjeeling

Arrival, Mall Road, rest. Acclimatisation day.

Day 2-3Compressed classic Darjeeling

Tiger Hill, 7-point tour, toy train. Stock up on trek essentials at Habeeb Mullick & local stores.

Day 4Drive to Maneybhanjyang + trek to Tumling

Morning drive (2 hours) to Maneybhanjyang. Permit check at the gate. Trek 11 km uphill to Tumling (5-6 hr, altitude gain 7,000 to 9,600 ft). Overnight at a trekkers' hut.

Stay: Tumling trekkers' hut (basic, ₹500-800)
Day 5Tumling to Kalipokhri

11 km moderate trek. Pass Tonglu, Gairibas. Reach Kalipokhri (the sacred "black lake"). Overnight at a basic guesthouse.

Day 6Kalipokhri to Sandakphu

7 km uphill - the steepest day of the trek. Reach Sandakphu (11,930 ft) - the highest peak in West Bengal. Sunset over Kanchenjunga + Everest + Lhotse + Makalu. Overnight in basic Sandakphu lodges.

Altitude: 11,930 ftStay: Sandakphu lodge (basic)
Day 7Sandakphu to Phalut (optional)

21 km ridge walk to Phalut. Most photographers' favourite day. Alternatively, return via Kalipokhri.

Day 8Phalut/Sandakphu to Gurdum or Rimbik

Long descent (16 km). End at Gurdum or Rimbik villages. Hot meals and proper beds finally.

Day 9Return to Darjeeling

Drive back from Rimbik to Darjeeling (3 hours). Rest day. Long shower. Big dinner at Glenary's. Sleep in a real bed.

Stay: Mall Road hotelEat: Glenary's celebration dinner
Day 10Departure

Slow morning, shared SUV to Bagdogra/NJP for afternoon flight or evening train onward.

09 · The long-stay traveller

Staying 20 days, a month, or longer? Read this.

For slow travellers, digital nomads & sabbatical takers

How to actually live in Darjeeling for a month

Honestly, Darjeeling is not Rishikesh or McLeodganj - the town itself is small and you'll exhaust the famous attractions in 4-5 days. But the surrounding villages are an entirely different story, and that's where you'll find your month-long life. The smart move is to base yourself in one offbeat village - Lamahatta, Tinchuley, Lepchajagat or Takdah - and rent a family homestay on monthly rates (₹25,000-40,000/month with three meals included, negotiable down further if you commit to 30+ days). Take long walks every morning. Pick a single tea estate and visit it weekly through different flushes - you'll understand the entire annual production cycle. Spend Tuesday-Thursday in the village, weekends in town for fast Wi-Fi, decent restaurants and a haircut.

For digital nomads with deadlines, choose Robertson Road or Jalapahar in Darjeeling town for the first two weeks - reliable Wi-Fi, multiple cafes (Revolver, The Buzz, Sonam's), Glenary's for client calls in a quiet upstairs nook. After two weeks, move to a village. The contrast will be everything you came for.

Plan some longer side circuits over the month - the full 5-7 day Sandakphu trek, a 4-day Sikkim circuit, a weekend at Mirik, an overnight in Bunkulung or Latpanchar for birdwatching. The Neora Valley fringe (Kolakham, Lava, Loleygaon) is a separate 3-day mini-circuit. Photography projects work brilliantly here - monsoon mists in August, rhododendrons in April, snow chances at Tiger Hill in January, the first-flush tea harvest in March-April.

Weather-based migration is the long-stay traveller's secret. From December to February, drop to lower-altitude offbeat villages like Bijanbari (2,500 ft) for warmer days. From April to June, stay in the higher villages like Lepchajagat or Chatakpur (6,900-7,887 ft) for cool relief. Avoid July-September if you can. The whole month-long Darjeeling experience is essentially this: own one local tea estate's annual cycle, build a relationship with one homestay family, and let the rest of the eastern Himalayas reveal itself slowly.

11 · The questions

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Darjeeling?
For first-time visitors, 3 days are ideal for the classic Darjeeling experience - Tiger Hill, 7-point tour, Mall Road and toy train. 4-5 days lets you add offbeat villages. 7 days combines Darjeeling with Sikkim. 10 days covers Darjeeling plus the Sandakphu-Phalut trek. Most travellers find 3-5 days the sweet spot. Less than 3 nights and you'll skip either the toy train, Happy Valley or any offbeat village.
What is the best Darjeeling itinerary?
The most-booked itinerary is the 3-day classic: Day 1 arrival and Mall Road, Day 2 Tiger Hill plus 7-point tour, Day 3 toy train plus Happy Valley plus Rock Garden. For couples and slow travellers, we recommend the 5-day plan which adds Mirik day trip and an offbeat homestay overnight (Lamahatta, Lepchajagat or Tinchuley) - this is our top pick.
Is 2 days enough for Darjeeling?
Two days are barely enough for the highlights. You can cover Tiger Hill sunrise, the 7-point tour and a Mall Road evening, but you'll have to skip the toy train, Happy Valley tea estate visit and any offbeat villages. We recommend at least 3 nights for a comfortable, unrushed experience.
Should I combine Darjeeling with Sikkim?
Yes - if you have 7 or more days. The Darjeeling-Gangtok-Tsomgo Lake circuit is one of the most popular combined itineraries in eastern India. 3 days in Darjeeling + 3-4 days in Sikkim covering MG Marg, Tsomgo Lake, Baba Harbhajan Mandir and Rumtek Monastery works perfectly. The drive between Darjeeling and Gangtok is 4-5 hours via the Teesta River route.
When should I plan the Sandakphu trek?
October-November and March-April are the best months for the 5-7 day Sandakphu-Phalut trek. Clear skies, manageable temperatures, no monsoon risk. Singalila trekking permits are issued only in these windows. December-February is technically open but bitterly cold with possible snow.
Can I do the Darjeeling itinerary as a solo traveller?
Absolutely. Darjeeling is one of the safest hill stations in India for solo travellers. Hostels like Zostel, Tripping Walrus and Hostelavie are social and well-located. The 7-point tour can be done via shared jeep for ₹200-300. Use the 3-day classic itinerary as your base, swap mid-range hotels for hostels, and add an offbeat village overnight for the trip's highlight.
Is the 7-day Darjeeling + Sikkim itinerary too rushed?
It's compact but doable. You'll do Darjeeling's classic 3-day plan (slightly accelerated), one transit day, and 3 full Sikkim days. If you want more depth, extend to 10 days and add Pelling or North Sikkim (Lachung/Yumthang). For Sikkim's North district, permits and bookings are mandatory in advance.
What's the best itinerary for honeymoon couples?
The 5-day plan with a tweak - upgrade hotels to heritage properties (Windamere, The Elgin, Mayfair or Cedar Inn), include the Windamere afternoon high tea on Day 1, and swap the Lamahatta night for a luxury tea-estate stay at Glenburn or Taj Chia Kutir. Most romantic stretch: the offbeat overnight on Day 5 with a candlelit homestay dinner in Tinchuley.
Can I stay in Darjeeling for a month?
Yes, but you'll need to base out of offbeat villages (Lamahatta, Tinchuley, Lepchajagat or Takdah) on monthly rates (₹25,000-40,000/month all-inclusive) rather than the main town. Two weeks in town followed by two weeks in a village is the smart split. Pair it with longer side circuits like Sandakphu trek, Sikkim, Neora Valley and Mirik to keep the month varied. Read the long-stay section above for details.
Is one day enough for Darjeeling?
Honestly, no. The 70 km uphill drive itself takes 3-3.5 hours each way. A one-day Darjeeling trip from Siliguri or Bagdogra means you only get 3-4 hours in town, enough for Tiger Hill sunrise OR Mall Road - not both. If you only have one day, do a Mirik day trip instead - it's lower altitude, shorter drive and a complete circuit.
What's the cheapest Darjeeling itinerary?
A budget 3-day itinerary works out to around ₹5,500-7,500 per person excluding flights. Use the Darjeeling Mail from Kolkata (sleeper class ₹600), shared SUVs (₹350-400), hostel dorms (₹600-900/night), eat at Hasty Tasty and Chowk Bazaar momo stalls (₹150-250/meal), do the shared-jeep Tiger Hill (₹250) instead of private cab. Total under ₹8,000 from Kolkata.
