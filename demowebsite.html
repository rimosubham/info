The real cost of missing one

What non-compliance actually costs

These are statutory penalties under the Companies Act, 2013. They accrue daily, they apply to the company and to officers in default, and they cannot be waived by apologising.

FilingDueIf you miss it
AOC-4 — Financial Statements30 days from AGM₹100 per day, no upper cap
MGT-7 / 7A — Annual Return60 days from AGM₹100 per day, no upper cap
DIR-3 KYC — Director KYC30 September annually₹5,000 and DIN deactivated
INC-20A — Commencement of Business180 days from incorporation₹50,000 company + ₹1,000/day officers
ADT-1 — Auditor Appointment15 days from AGMEscalating penalty, appointment challenged
DPT-3 — Return of Deposits30 June annuallyPenalty plus officer liability

Indicative figures for illustration. Actual liability depends on entity type, delay period and the provisions in force. We'll confirm your exact exposure in the free health check.

Before you call

Questions we get every week

?

Is the compliance health check really free?

Yes. We review your public MCA filing history, director KYC status and charge register, and send you a one-page summary. There's no fee and no obligation to engage us afterwards.

?

We've missed filings for two or three years. Is that fixable?

Almost always. Back-year filings can be made with additional fees, and where penalties have been levied there are compounding and adjudication routes. It gets more expensive the longer it waits — but it is very rarely unfixable.

?

Do you work with companies outside Bengaluru and Chennai?

Yes. MCA filings are entirely online, so location is rarely a constraint. Our offices are where we meet clients — not where our work is limited to.

?

How do you charge — retainer or per filing?

Both are available. Most ongoing clients prefer an annual retainer covering the full compliance calendar, since it's predictable and cheaper than transaction-by-transaction. One-off work is quoted as a fixed fee before we start.

?

Can you handle both the CS and the CA side?

That's the entire point of the firm. Corporate secretarial, accounting, audit and taxation sit under one roof, under one engagement partner — so the tax position and the ROC position are decided together rather than in two separate conversations.
