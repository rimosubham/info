A short story about your stack

You run on tools
you don't track.

The rise of AI broke the SaaS rulebook. What was once a single all-in-one suite has fractured into a galaxy of specialised tools. Every founder, freelancer, and team is now stitched together by a stack of subscriptions — each with its own roadmap, its own pricing team, its own quiet "we've adjusted our plans" email.

They don't all raise prices on the same day. They don't all raise prices at all — some just remove features and call it a "plan refresh". And nobody, anywhere, is keeping a single ledger of what actually changed.

Until now.

How we predict

Not magic.
Just math.

We don't guess when prices will rise. We model it. PricePulse runs four signal streams on every tool we track — fed by daily scrapes, public filings, and behavioural data — to forecast pricing moves before the email lands in your inbox.

SIGNAL 01
📈

Pricing cadence

Every tool has a rhythm. We map every previous change — the month, the magnitude, the plan tier targeted — to predict the shape of the next one. Vendors who hiked twice in 24 months almost always hike a third time.

Source · Daily scrape diff
SIGNAL 02
💼

Financial pressure

Public earnings, recent funding rounds, layoffs, IPO readiness, and gross-margin trends all show up in pricing 4–9 months later. We feed every public filing into the model.

Source · 10-Q, Crunchbase, news
SIGNAL 03
🎤

Leadership behaviour

CEOs telegraph price changes. Public statements about "value capture", senior hiring in finance teams, board changes, and earnings-call language all correlate with pricing moves within two quarters.

Source · Earnings transcripts, LinkedIn
SIGNAL 04
🌊

Market pressure

A category-wide hike makes laggards follow. We watch every competitor, every adjacent tool, every benchmark. When 3 vendors in a category move, the fourth almost always does within 90 days.

Source · Competitive set scan
