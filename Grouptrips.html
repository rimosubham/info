How ours are different

Small groups. Real conversations.

Big group tours are efficient. They're also exhausting, impersonal, and forgettable. Here's why ours actually work.

Max 12. Always.

We've walked behind 50-person groups. Never again. 12 people max means conversations happen at meals, everyone knows everyone's name, and the van doesn't need a parking lot.

We've walked every trip

Every group trip is a route we've run at least 4 times already. The homestays know us. The guides are our friends. Nothing is improvised.

Built-in nothing days

Half of every itinerary is unstructured. Read, nap, wander, skip a meal. The best moments happen when we're not trying to make them.

I'm not great with strangers. Should I skip group trips?

Most of our travelers say the same thing before they come. And every single one ends up in the WhatsApp group after. We pair introverts together. You can eat alone, skip meals, or join in — nobody will push.

Can I bring my partner / friend?

Yes, and we encourage it. Group 4+ gets the 5th spot free. Many groups end up being a mix of couples, friend pairs, and solos — which is often the best mix.

What's the refund policy?

₹5,000 reservation is fully refundable within 14 days. After that: full refund until 45 days before, 70% until 21 days, 30% until 7 days. Medical emergency? Always full credit, no questions.

Are children allowed?

Most group trips are 18+. We're working on a family-focused departure for 2027 — sign up via the newsletter for early access.

What's included in the price?

All accommodation, local transport from pickup point, all meals except 2 "explore on your own" dinners, guide fees, insurance, permits, entry fees. Not included: flights, alcohol, souvenir shopping.
