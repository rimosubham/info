Canva.in

Building canva.in from zero authority to DR 70 in 12 months — across 12+ Indian languages, with no link exchanges allowed.

When Canva launched its first country-specific TLD, we had to invent four new playbooks: telephonic outreach to regional magazines, brand-mention plays instead of links, multilingual campaigns across Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and more — all with a strict no-quid-pro-quo policy.

Spocket

5×'d Spocket's traffic with 1,600 backlinks and zero agency spend — surviving two Google core updates along the way.

Spocket's on-page SEO was world-class but their backlink profile was holding them back. We replaced expensive vendors with four organic campaigns: Slack outreach, backlink gap analysis, broken-link recovery, and the "drafts strategy" that intercepts placements before articles publish.
