What if I don't know where I want to go?

Excellent. Those are the trips we love most.

Just fill the form with what kind of experience you're after — quiet, slow, adventurous, solo, whatever — and we'll come back with 2–3 destination options we think will fit. Then you pick.

Is the ₹1,500 fee refundable if I don't book?

You pay the ₹1,500 after you see our initial plan and approve it. If the plan doesn't resonate, we'll revise once for free or you can walk away — no fee charged.

Once you pay, the ₹1,500 is fully credited against any upgrade (Full Plan or Concierge).

How many revisions do I get?

Self-Guide: 1 free revision.
Full Plan: Unlimited until you approve.
Concierge: Unlimited, plus a 1:1 call anytime.

Can I book flights through you?

We don't book flights — you'll always get better rates direct. But we'll recommend the best airports, routes, and timings in your itinerary, and tell you which dates will save you money.

What if something goes wrong during my trip?

We're on WhatsApp during your entire trip. Roads close, weather turns — we've rebooked travelers 43 times in the last 18 months at zero extra cost. Zero travelers stranded.

Can my group of 6 split across different stays?

Yes. We do this often for big groups — one family in a heritage stay, friends in a homestay nearby. We plan the splits and the daytime overlaps. Works well for multi-generational trips.
