Where to Stay in Darjeeling: Best Hotels, Heritage Stays, Hostels & Homestays

From ₹600 hostel dorms to ₹25,000 heritage suites - the complete accommodation guide, sorted by area, budget and the kind of traveller you are.

Darjeeling has roughly 600 listed hotels and homestays, which is wonderful in theory and overwhelming in practice. Some sit on Mall Road with a 90-second walk to a Glenary's croissant. Others are forty minutes uphill on Jalapahar where the air is colder, quieter and the Kanchenjunga view comes straight out of your window. There are colonial heritage hotels from 1841 still serving afternoon tea with silver tongs, modern 3-stars with rooftop restaurants, friendly hostels with fairy lights and pour-over coffee, and tea-estate homestays where you'll wake up to a rooster instead of a city. This guide breaks every option down by area, budget and traveller type - so you can scroll straight to the section that matters and skip the rest.

01 · By area

Pick your area first - then the hotel

Darjeeling is built across ridges and steep slopes. A "10-minute walk" on a map can feel like 30 minutes on foot if you're walking uphill with luggage. Picking the right neighbourhood matters more than picking the perfect hotel.

The 8 areas below cover where 95% of all Darjeeling accommodation sits. Each has a distinct vibe, price point and walkability profile.

The heart of town

Mall Road & Chowrasta

₹3,000 - 25,000

Pedestrian-only square at the social centre of Darjeeling. Heritage hotels, cafes, bookstores and souvenir shops are within 2-5 minutes on foot. Evening street musicians, pony rides for kids, and sunset Kanchenjunga views from Observatory Hill nearby.

Best for: First-time visitors, families with kids, older travellers, honeymooners
Modern & central

Gandhi Road

₹2,000 - 12,000

The main commercial road, 5-10 minutes walk from Mall Road. Most modern mid-range hotels (Ramada, Sumitel, Udaan, Sumi Yashshree) sit here. Good restaurants, ATMs, pharmacies and connectivity. Walk-friendly with vehicle access right to the door.

Best for: Couples, business travellers, families wanting modern comforts
Quiet & central

Robertson Road

₹1,500 - 6,000

Just behind Gandhi Road. Quieter at night, mid-range hotels and budget guesthouses. 10-12 minute walk to Mall Road. Popular hostels (Tripping Walrus, Hostelavie) operate here. Less commercial chaos.

Best for: Solo travellers, bachelors, light sleepers, budget couples
Walkable & relaxed

C.R. Das & Zakir Hussain Road

₹2,000 - 8,000

Boutique mid-range hotels with heritage character. 7-10 minute walk to Mall. Less foot traffic, lots of mountain-view rooms. Sonam's Kitchen and several quiet local cafes here. Good middle ground.

Best for: Honeymooners on mid-budget, slow travellers
Upper ridge serenity

Jalapahar

₹5,000 - 18,000

Above the main town, on the upper ridge. The quietest stay area in Darjeeling. Many heritage and boutique properties (Cedar Inn, Windamere extension cottages) sit here. 15-20 minute downhill walk to Mall - which means uphill on the way back. Best Kanchenjunga views.

Best for: Honeymooners, photographers, peace-seekers
Tea estate stays

Lebong & Tea Estate Belt

₹6,000 - 30,000

4-6 km below Mall Road, this is where the working tea estates are. Glenburn Tea Estate, Makaibari Bungalow and Puttabong Cottage offer working-plantation stays. Beautiful but you'll need a cab for every trip into town.

Best for: Tea enthusiasts, honeymooners, long-stay travellers
Slow & old-world

Ghoom & Sonada

₹1,500 - 9,000

8-10 km from Darjeeling, near the toy train route. Quiet, slower-paced and cheaper. Sterling Darjeeling is the marquee property here. You'll need a cab for Mall Road every time, but mornings at Ghoom Monastery are unforgettable.

Best for: Senior travellers, those returning for a quieter second visit
Forest village homestays

Offbeat (Lamahatta, Lepchajagat, Tinchuley)

₹1,500 - 4,000

23-32 km from Darjeeling town. Forest hamlets with family-run homestays, no Wi-Fi in many cases, home-cooked Nepali meals included. The slow-travel option - not where you stay if your itinerary is "sightseeing-only".

Best for: Couples, solo travellers, writers, slow-travel families
Our recommendation
For first-time visitors: book a mid-range mountain-facing hotel near Mall Road or Gandhi Road for the first two nights, then move to an offbeat homestay in Lamahatta, Lepchajagat or Tinchuley for the third night. The contrast between buzzing town and silent forest is what makes the trip memorable.

03 · By traveller type

The right stay for your kind of trip

Different travellers want different things from the same town. Here's the matching stay by who you are.

✂
Solo travellers & bachelors

Hostels on Robertson Road

Zostel, Tripping Walrus or Hostelavie. Social atmosphere, female-only dorms available, in-house cafes and tour booking help. ₹600-1,200/night.

✿
Couples (mid-budget)

3-star Kanchenjunga-view rooms

Sumi Yashshree, Summit Grace, Udaan Dekeling, Hotel Sonar Bangla. Request north-facing rooms. ₹3,500-5,500/night with breakfast included.

♛
Honeymooners & newlyweds

Heritage suites with fireplaces

Windamere, The Elgin, Mayfair, Glenburn Tea Estate or Cedar Inn. Candlelit dinners, valley views, vintage decor. ₹10K-25K/night.

⚔
Bachelor / friend groups (4-8)

Villa-style 3 or 4-bedroom rentals

StayVista, Airbnb, Saffron Stays full-property villas. ₹12K-25K total per night for the whole group. Better than booking 4 hotel rooms.

🏠
Families with young kids

Mid-range hotels with elevators

Ramada by Wyndham, Sumitel JB Park, Sterling Darjeeling. In-room dining, 24-hr room service, no steep stair climbs. ₹5K-10K/night.

☀
Travelling with parents / older travellers

Ground-floor or elevator-equipped

Hotel Seven Seventeen, Central Heritage Resort & Spa, Sinclairs Darjeeling. Walking-distance to Mall, low stairs, accessible bathrooms.

⚙
Digital nomads / long-stay

Homestays with strong Wi-Fi

Look for "Wi-Fi confirmed" homestays in Jalapahar or Robertson Road area. Some heritage properties (Cedar Inn) offer monthly long-stay rates.

⛰
Slow-travel & offbeat seekers

Forest village homestays

Lamahatta, Lepchajagat, Tinchuley, Chatakpur. Home-cooked meals included, no Wi-Fi, deep forest silence. ₹1,500-3,500/night with all meals.

Luxury & heritage hotels in Darjeeling

The top of the Darjeeling stay pyramid. Heritage colonial properties, working tea-estate bungalows, and the rare 5-star with full spa. Best for honeymoons, anniversaries, and the once-in-a-while splurge.

The oldest hotel in Darjeeling and arguably the most atmospheric stay in eastern India. Originally a lodging for British tea planters, it still serves a 4 PM afternoon tea with silver tongs and white-gloved waiters. 09 · The smart moves

Booking timing & smart-saver tips

When to book

For peak season (April-May, October-November, December last week), book hotels 4-6 weeks ahead. Heritage hotels sell out 2-3 months ahead during Durga Puja week and Christmas-NY. For shoulder months (June, September, late January), 2-3 weeks ahead is usually enough.

When NOT to lock in non-refundable rates

Monsoon season (June-September) - keep your booking refundable. Landslides can shut roads for 12-48 hours and you might need to push your dates. Off-season prices already low - you don't save much locking in.

Mountain-view rooms - the trick

Always request north-facing rooms in writing when you book. Most hotels have only 30-50% of rooms with proper Kanchenjunga view. The same hotel-room category can have wildly different views depending on which floor and which side.

What to confirm before booking

  • Heating in winter - many older Darjeeling hotels have only one or two heated rooms. Ask specifically.
  • Hot water reliability - geyser timings, not "available on request"
  • Vehicle access - some Mall Road / Jalapahar properties need a 100-300 metre walk from the nearest road. Critical for elderly travellers.
  • Wi-Fi quality - "Wi-Fi available" can mean lobby-only. Get specific.
  • Mountain view guarantee - if booking for the view, get this in writing or specific room number
Honest take
For a 3-day Darjeeling trip, mix-and-match works best. 2 nights in a mid-range hotel near Mall Road (for the convenience), then 1 night in an offbeat homestay in Lamahatta or Tinchuley (for the experience). Total cost: ₹8K-12K per person. The contrast is what makes the trip memorable.

11 · The questions

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best area to stay in Darjeeling?
For first-time visitors, stay within 1 km of Mall Road / Chowrasta - including Gandhi Road, C.R. Das Road, Robertson Road and Dr. Zakir Hussain Road. You'll be walking distance to cafes, restaurants and shopping. For honeymooners and quiet stays, Jalapahar upper ridge is excellent. For tea-estate experiences, Lebong, Ghoom and Kurseong are the dedicated zones.
Which is the best luxury hotel in Darjeeling?
The most acclaimed luxury and heritage hotels are Windamere Hotel (1841), The Elgin, Mayfair Darjeeling, Taj Chia Kutir Resort & Spa (in nearby Kurseong) and Glenburn Tea Estate. Rates range ₹10K-30K per night. Mayfair is most family-friendly, Glenburn most romantic, Windamere most heritage-immersive.
Which is the best budget hotel in Darjeeling?
Top-rated budget hotels are Hotel Pine Crest Inn, Kaskilay, The Revett and Hotel Broadway. For hostels, Zostel Darjeeling, Tripping Walrus, Hostelavie and Hidden Monkey Backpackers offer dorms from ₹600-900 per night and private rooms from ₹1,200-1,800.
Is a homestay better than a hotel in Darjeeling?
Homestays are better for offbeat experience and lower budgets. Hotels are better for convenience, predictable service and being close to Mall Road. For first-time visitors we recommend 2 nights in a Mall Road hotel followed by 1 night in an offbeat homestay (Lamahatta, Tinchuley or Lepchajagat) to get the best of both worlds.
Are there hotels in Darjeeling with Kanchenjunga view?
Yes. The best Kanchenjunga-view properties are Cedar Inn, Mayfair Darjeeling, Summit Grace, Sumi Yashshree Mall Road, Sterling Darjeeling (Ghoom) and Sumitel JB Park. Always request a north-facing room in writing - not every room of the same category has the view.
How much does a hotel in Darjeeling cost?
Budget hostels & guesthouses: ₹600-1,500. Budget hotels: ₹1,500-3,000. Mid-range 3-star: ₹3,000-6,000. Premium 4-star & boutique: ₹6,000-12,000. Heritage 5-star: ₹12,000-30,000. Add 30-60% for peak season (April-May, Oct-Nov, Christmas-NY week).
Which hotel is best for honeymoon in Darjeeling?
For honeymoon, the top picks are Windamere Hotel, The Elgin, Mayfair Darjeeling, Glenburn Tea Estate and Cedar Inn. For mid-budget honeymoons, Summit Grace and Sumitel JB Park are excellent. Book heritage hotels at least 8-10 weeks in advance during October-November and April-May.
Are there hotels in Darjeeling for large groups (10+ people)?
Yes. For large groups, look at full-property villa rentals on StayVista, Airbnb and Saffron Stays - typically ₹12K-25K per night for the whole property. Larger 3-4 star hotels like Sumitel JB Park, Ramada by Wyndham and Sterling Darjeeling can also offer block bookings with group discounts. Always negotiate directly for groups of 8 or more.
Are pet-friendly stays available in Darjeeling?
Pet-friendly options are limited but they exist. Several Lamahatta and Tinchuley homestays welcome pets. Cedar Inn permits pets in specific cottages with prior approval. Always confirm in writing before booking - many luxury heritage hotels do not allow pets at all.
When should I book Darjeeling hotels?
For peak season (April-May, October-November, December last week), book 4-6 weeks ahead minimum. Heritage hotels sell out 2-3 months ahead for Durga Puja week and Christmas-New Year. For shoulder months (June, September, late January), 2-3 weeks ahead is sufficient.
Do Darjeeling hotels have heating in winter?
Heritage hotels and most 4-5 star properties have heating, fireplaces or electric heaters. Many mid-range and budget hotels offer only blankets and quilts. If you're travelling December-February, always confirm heating availability in writing before booking. Nighttime temperatures can drop to 1-4°C.
Are there any government guest houses in Darjeeling?
Yes. The Darjeeling Tourist Lodge, Kurseong Tourist Lodge, Mirik Tourist Lodge, Dafey Munal Tourist Lodge, Jorepokhari Tourist Lodge and Lowis Jubilee Sanitarium are all West Bengal government-run options. Rates ₹1,500-3,500. Book directly through the West Bengal Tourism Development Corporation website. Quality varies by property - read recent reviews before booking.
