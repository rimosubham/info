Why travelers stay for seconds

Other sites sell tours. We share the moments that change how you travel forever.

A "travel experience" on most websites is a scripted performance — a staged cultural show, a curated photo spot, a guide who's given the same lecture 2,000 times. It's travel as theater, with you as the audience.

Our experiences are the opposite. They're quiet, imperfect, and uncurated. A homestay host who forgets a detail and then insists on showing you her grandmother's recipe notebook. A trek guide who grew up on that trail, not one trained for it. A monastery breakfast where the monks are actually doing their morning prayers, not waiting for your camera.

This is travel as it was before Instagram. And once you've done it, the other kind feels embarrassingly hollow.

How do I know the homestays and guides are actually authentic?

Short answer: we've personally stayed at every homestay and done every trail before we listed it. No exceptions.

Long answer: each host is in our WhatsApp groups, we re-visit the properties every 9–12 months, and 68% of our travelers stay in touch with their host after the trip. If a host doesn't live up to what we wrote, we don't just refund — we delist.

Can I combine multiple experiences into one trip?

Yes — and most travelers do. Check the "better together" stacks above for our top three combinations, or use our custom itinerary service (from ₹1,500) and we'll build a trip that layers whichever experiences you want.

What if I cancel? What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to 30 days before. 70% between 30–15 days. 30% between 15–7 days. Under 7 days — we hold your booking as credit for up to 12 months. We're small and we won't penalise you unfairly.

Are experiences suitable for solo women travelers?

Several of them are built specifically for solo women — the Uttarakhand stack, for example, uses only female-run homestays and includes our local guardian network (a verified contact in every town you visit).

We've hosted 186 solo women travelers in the last 18 months. Zero incidents. We take this seriously.

How early should I book?

Festivals and adventure sports: 3–6 months ahead (Losar and Manali–Leh cycling sell out 9 months in advance).

Homestays and workation stays: 4–8 weeks ahead, longer in peak season (Oct–Feb, Apr–Jun).

Custom itineraries: Minimum 2 weeks notice, though we've turned one around in 5 days for an emergency honeymoon once.

Do I get to talk to a real person before booking?

Yes. Every booking includes a free 20-minute WhatsApp or voice call where we walk you through the plan, answer questions, and recommend changes based on your travel style. No pushy sales scripts — our average call length is about 12 minutes.
