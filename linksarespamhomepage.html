The Spam
Glossary.

Words you'll hear us say. Now you'll know what they mean.

PBN
A Private Blog Network. Translation: a fake blog farm built to game Google. Translation of that translation: a Google penalty waiting to happen. We don't touch them.
White-hat SEO
Playing by Google's rules. Boring name. Best results. Less risky than your morning coffee.
Toxic link
A link from a site so sketchy that Google penalises you for owning it. We find them. We help you disavow them.
Link juice
The SEO power passed from one page to another. Yes, that's the actual industry term. Yes, we know how that sounds.
